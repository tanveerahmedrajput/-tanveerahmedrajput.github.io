Featured project

Smart Advertising using Augmented Reality

An Android application that turns any billboard or printed hoarding into a live video ad. Point the camera at a recognised image, and the app detects it in real time and plays the matching advertisement over it — a direct, hands-on view of physical and digital worlds overlapping.

Built as my final-year thesis at Mehran University using Vuforia for image recognition and Android Studio for the app itself — the same instinct for overlaying structure on top of something real that still shows up in how I plan a lesson.
